Find your size

Every ready-made piece is cut to this chart. Measure over light clothing and keep the tape snug, not tight.

  • A
    ShoulderStraight across the back, from one shoulder edge to the other.
  • B
    BustAround the fullest part of the chest, tape parallel to the floor.
  • C
    WaistAround the natural waistline, just above the navel.
  • D
    HipAround the fullest part of the hips, roughly 8" below the waist.
  • E
    LengthFrom the shoulder point straight down to where you want the hem to fall.

Tip: measure with a soft tape, stand naturally, and take numbers over light clothing rather than bulky layers — it's the easiest way to avoid a returned order.

About the house

The short version, for anywhere you list us online.

Modern Villa is a women's ready-to-wear showroom built around one idea: festive doesn't have to mean uncomfortable, and comfortable doesn't have to mean plain. We stock three lines — suits for traditional functions, nightsuits for home, and cord sets for the days in between — and every piece is finished and ready to wear off the rack.

We work directly with our tailoring floor, so sizing stays consistent from the first piece you buy to the fiftieth, and we can turn around custom lengths and repeat orders quickly.

Everything from grading the size chart to choosing this season's cuts happens in-house, which is also why we're comfortable standing behind fit and finish on every order.
